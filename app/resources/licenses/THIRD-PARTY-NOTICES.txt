Replacing it
------------
EnzyDock looks for the docking engine in this order: a path you set in
Preferences, the ENZYDOCK_VINA environment variable, this bundled copy,
the folder holding the application, then the system PATH.  To use a
different build, either drop it into the folder above under the name
EnzyDock expects on your platform ("vina.exe" on Windows,
"vina_mac_aarch64" or "vina_mac_x86_64" on macOS), or set it in
Preferences.  On macOS a binary downloaded from the internet may need
`xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine <file>` before it will run.
